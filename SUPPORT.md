# NanoVision Support

**Welcome to NanoVision Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of
your media optimization toolkit.

## Supported Platforms

NanoVision is available on the following Apple platforms:

- **iOS 26+** — iPhone
- **iPadOS 26+** — iPad
- **visionOS 26+** — Apple Vision Pro
- **macOS 26+** — Mac

## What NanoVision Does

NanoVision is a suite of on-device tools for shrinking and editing your media —
all processed locally, with nothing uploaded:

- **Image** — compress and optimize photos without sending them anywhere.
- **Audio** — smart-transcode audio to smaller files while keeping quality.
- **PDF** — a unified editor for organizing, compressing, and reworking PDFs.
- **Watermark** — add live-preview watermarks to your images.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I compress a file?** Pick a tool from the home screen, add your file,
adjust the settings if you like, then run the compression. Your optimized file
is saved wherever you choose.

**Where are my optimized files saved?** Wherever you tell the App to save them.
NanoVision never keeps its own copy.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does NanoVision collect?** None. Every file is processed entirely on
your device, with no network upload, no accounts, and no analytics. For full
details, see our Privacy Policy.

**Does NanoVision need an internet connection?** No. The App works fully offline.

**How do I delete my data?** NanoVision stores nothing of its own. Simply delete
any files you created and/or remove the App from your device.

### Troubleshooting

**The output file is not smaller than the original.** Some files are already
optimally compressed, so re-compressing them can't shrink them further.
NanoVision detects this and avoids producing a file that is larger than the
original.

**The app is crashing or not responding.**

1. Force-quit the app and reopen it.
2. Ensure your device is running the latest OS version.
3. Update NanoVision to the latest version from the App Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).

**A file fails to open or process.** Make sure the file isn't corrupted and is a
format the tool supports. Try a different file to confirm whether the issue is
file-specific.
